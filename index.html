About Me

I'm Sowndarya Chockalingam, I'm 19 years old, I'm a first-year AI Systems and Machine Learning major at Drexel University. I'm taking this course because the job market for AI/ML is flourishing right now. I've always been interested in software engineering and CS, but I chose AI since the field is ‘taking’ entry-level jobs in software engineering and CS. I'm still exploring my options, possibly double-majoring in cybersecurity as well. My main goal is to develop practical skills like Python and other programming languages that I can build on my resume. A real moment where AI stuck with me was during high school when a student recommended an AI tool designed for Caribbean Advanced Placement Exams. Since only a small population takes these exams, the resources are so hard to find, but this tool brought everything in one place, including practice papers, past exams, and a chatbot to help solve problems. Having all that accessible really helped me through my last two years of high school. My biggest concerns with AI are the environmental impact and how easily it spreads misinformation, especially to people like my grandparents who trust almost everything they see on the internet. I think I'm the most curious about Outcome 4, Machine Learning Approaches, how AI learns and figures out which approach to use to solve a problem. This matters to me personally because I'm curious about how AI learns almost like a human and also its thought process when it doesn't know what to do when given a specific problem. Details about me are I love playing pickleball even though I'm not the best at it, I'm from Jamaica and therefore also an international student and I love love reading.
